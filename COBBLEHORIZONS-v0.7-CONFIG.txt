CobbleHorizons v0.7 Balance & Cleanup configuration profile

Core goals:
- 1.25x global Pokemon XP (down from 2.0x)
- modern 1/4096 base shiny rate
- RCT trainer density reduced while keeping on-sight battles and level caps
- no CobbleDollars from wild Pokemon
- emerald-to-CobbleDollars bank conversion disabled
- Master Ball and Ancient Origin Ball removed from standard shop
- PokeNav hides unknown species until discovered
- breeding slowed and limited to 3 active pastures
- TM/TR passive drop chance reduced to 5%
- Tera/Stellar shard drops reduced
- legendary nest chances reduced
- moon-themed legendary summons require correct moon phase
- wild Waystones less common and teleport XP cost capped at 12
- MAmbience footsteps disabled to eliminate modded-block footstep log spam

All original configuration files not explicitly changed were preserved.
Local cache/user files were removed from the distributable profile.
